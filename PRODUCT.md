# AssetFlow landing

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Households organizing ownership records and maintenance; businesses, vehicle-fleet operators, and hotel teams evaluating future editions.

## Product Purpose
Help visitors recognize their asset-management needs and understand how AssetFlow supports a reviewable maintenance plan and useful ownership history.

## Capabilities and Constraints
AssetFlow Home is available at https://myassetsflow.app. Business, Fleet, and Hospitality are Coming Soon. QR labels and professional role-based workflows in the articles are future-edition previews. AI maintenance drafts require human review. Calendar export is available; connected-calendar synchronization is planned.

The homepage leads with Home and an illustrative ownership example: asset, maintenance-plan and records tabs, followed by a local review, confirm and reset interaction. Confirmation creates no real reminder and sends no external data. Without JavaScript, all three example panels remain readable. The market matrix labels Home as available and Business, Fleet and Hospitality as planned editions. Edition-specific icon marks pair each AssetFlow product name in the picker and table; selecting an edition highlights its full column. A dedicated localized blog index links to complete articles.

“How AssetFlow works” expands into three chapters: Add an asset, Build its knowledge base and Plan its care. Each chapter shows two visible illustrative examples, side by side on desktop and stacked on mobile, with explanatory captions. A linked overview navigates between chapters; contextual links select the relevant existing ownership-demo tab, lead to the library section or open a localized guide. The examples connect the same vehicle's details, documents, service history, reviewable maintenance draft and suggested next steps. All chapter content supports the six site languages, including Arabic RTL and Arabic-Indic numerals; the examples remain readable without JavaScript.

The dedicated library section presents connected records as a knowledge base: source evidence, understandable history and suggested tasks or maintenance plans for human review. It does not imply that proposed document extraction or automatic deadline updates are delivered.

The household section retains “One home. One shared picture.” and explains shared history, responsibility for tasks and the shared household plan through three benefits and an illustrative work board. The person/plus member row is decorative; the plus does not invite a member. The plan-seat note remains explicit: the owner is included, Free has no additional seats, and Plus, Pro and Max allow 1, 2 and 4 additional members respectively.

A separate asset-category section follows household sharing and precedes pricing. Six colored SVG icons and descriptions explain the relevant record and care context for Home & property, Electronics, Vehicles, Furniture, Appliances and Other. Examples follow the app's asset field schema: property responsibilities, device warranty and backup checks, vehicle mileage and service history, furniture material and exposure, appliance type/model and recurring care, and custom records. These are contextual examples, with no promises of automatic compliance, diagnosis or universal maintenance schedules. Both sections ship in all six locales, including Arabic RTL and localized numerals; household members and tasks are illustrative.

The landing repository is a dependency-free static website, independent of the app, worker, database, and infrastructure repositories. Content supports English, Italian, German, Spanish, French, and Arabic, including RTL and Arabic-Indic numerals.

## Brand Commitments
The website uses AssetFlow forest green and gold with a more vivid editorial palette of sky blue and sage. Georgia headlines and local Inter body text remain; Arabic uses native Tahoma/Arial stacks, RTL layout and Arabic-Indic numerals. Preserve the existing AssetFlow logos. Lead with the ownership interface and keep realistic photography subordinate inside its illustrative asset view; never imply it is a live app screenshot or customer evidence. Clear, practical language shows the customer as the person in control.

## Evidence on Hand
The existing landing copy and product documentation establish Home workflows. Article scenarios and records are illustrative, not testimonials or shipped product screenshots. Do not invent quantified savings, compliance guarantees, recovered records, or maintenance intervals.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic, keyboard-accessible pages; complete static article text without JavaScript; mobile reading and diagrams; localized labels and meaningful text alternatives.
