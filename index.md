# AssetFlow Home

AssetFlow Home is an application for organizing personal and household assets
and staying ahead of maintenance. Examples include appliances, electronics,
vehicles, equipment, and home systems.

## Ownership workflow

1. Add an asset with its basic details.
2. Create a suggested maintenance plan using AI.
3. Review the draft and confirm the tasks that fit your needs.
4. Follow maintenance tasks and reminders.
5. Keep completed work, documents, and service history together.

AI suggestions are reviewable drafts. They are not automatic verification of
an asset's condition or a replacement for the manufacturer's instructions.

## Documents and household collaboration

Keep receipts, manuals, asset documents, and evidence linked to completed work.
These records form an asset knowledge base that users can ask about to find
details and understand history. Household sharing is available according to
the selected plan; the owner manages invitations and billing.

## Calendar

The current workflow supports an iCalendar (.ics) download. Connected calendar
synchronization is being prepared.

## Editions and languages

AssetFlow Home is active. AssetFlow Business, Hospitality, and Fleet are coming
soon. Supported languages are English, Italian, German, Spanish, French, and
Arabic. The public landing page switches languages through its selector.
