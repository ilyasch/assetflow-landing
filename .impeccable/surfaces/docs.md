# Product documentation

Mode: Read. Audience: people evaluating AssetFlow Home and customers who need to understand a product workflow. Success: find a practical, concise guide to adding an asset, organizing records, or planning maintenance, then return to the product or explore another guide.

The documentation hub and guides are separate from the editorial blog. Product links lead here. The first release contains three complete guides in English, Italian, German, Spanish, French, and Arabic. Use the established AssetFlow editorial world, reading measure, semantic contents navigation, responsive behavior, and Arabic RTL. Every page has a localized index, language navigation, accessible headings, and useful metadata. Product guidance must reflect actual Home behavior; maintenance suggestions are drafts for human review, and manufacturer guidance remains the source for asset-specific schedules.
