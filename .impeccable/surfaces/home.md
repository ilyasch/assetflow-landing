# Homepage redesign

Mode: Persuade. Audience: households organizing ownership records and maintenance. Primary action: start free at myassetsflow.app. Six languages, including Arabic RTL. Website only; the app is outside the write boundary.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Less to remember, more peace of mind. A short opening and an explorable ownership example replace the long checklist and repeated feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: LangWatch-inspired clarity, confidence, and color, interpreted through AssetFlow's forest green and gold with sky blue and sage. Georgia serif display, local Inter body, native Arabic stacks. A floating pill top bar, clear section rail on wide screens, slow animated color wash, product-led visual and spacious link-column footer. Realistic editorial photography stays inside the crisp ownership interface; no hand-drawn or childlike vehicle art. Fine rules, solid pill controls and open editorial sections.

STORY: Recognize familiar household ownership, explore an asset, review a suggested task, retain records, then start free. Home leads; a market matrix compares Home, Business, Fleet and Hospitality by audience, asset focus, maintenance, records and collaboration. Future columns explicitly describe planned editions and capabilities.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating pill navigation above a clear, large serif headline and concise copy, visible Start free and secondary demonstration link. A slow gold/sky/sage color wash keeps the space lively without suggesting retail; reduced-motion preferences stop it. On wide screens, the left section rail tracks the current part of the page. Directly below, the wide product interface carries the visual weight: dark green navigation, colorful status details, a real asset photograph, and the maintenance/records interaction. The footer closes with distinct Product, Solutions and Resources columns.

FORM: Build direction refined from the open landing redesign: retain the page story and behavior while increasing color, photographic realism and interface craft. Signature interaction: explore ownership tabs, review and confirm a sample task, reset locally. Subtle blur-to-clear tab transitions; reduced motion disables them.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

The vehicle photograph is generated and its provenance is recorded in `assets/IMAGE-SOURCES.md`. Existing logos and social images retain their sources. Demonstration content is explicitly illustrative. No testimonials, quantified savings, new product claims, or external data writes.
