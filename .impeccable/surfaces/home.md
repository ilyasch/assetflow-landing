# Homepage redesign

Mode: Persuade. Audience: households organizing ownership records and maintenance. Primary action: start free at myassetsflow.app. Six languages, including Arabic RTL. Website only; the app is outside the write boundary.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Less to remember, more peace of mind. A short opening and an explorable ownership example replace the long checklist and repeated feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: LangWatch-inspired spacious composition, warm white, forest green and restrained gold. Georgia serif display, local Inter body, native Arabic stacks. Fine rules, solid pill controls, one elevated product demonstration, open editorial sections.

STORY: Recognize familiar household ownership, explore an asset, review a suggested task, retain records, then start free. Home leads; a market matrix compares Home, Business, Fleet and Hospitality by audience, asset focus, maintenance, records and collaboration. Future columns explicitly describe planned editions and capabilities.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact navigation above a large left-aligned two-line serif headline, concise supporting copy, visible Start free and secondary demonstration link. A green/gold atmospheric field balances the whitespace. The wide product example starts just below, with a dark green tab rail and a light asset/maintenance/records stage.

FORM: Approved plan, code-led replacement using the user's explicit LangWatch reference and confirmed choices. No concept lottery: the visual direction and page structure are pinned by the approved plan. Signature interaction: explore ownership tabs, review and confirm a sample task, reset locally. Subtle blur-to-clear tab transitions; reduced motion disables them.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

No new raster images. Existing logos and social images retain their sources. Demonstration content is explicitly illustrative. No testimonials, quantified savings, new product claims, or external data writes.
