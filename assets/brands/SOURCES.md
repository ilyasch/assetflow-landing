# Assistant and protocol marks

These marks identify examples of assistants and a connection protocol. They are
not an endorsement or a compatibility certification. No logo is loaded remotely
by the published page. Original proportions and colors are retained.

- `chatgpt.svg`: unmodified black OpenAI Blossom, including its supplied clear
  space. Reused from the app's existing reviewed asset. Official source:
  https://openai.com/brand/ and https://cdn.openai.com/brand/openai-logos.zip.
- `claude.png`: unmodified Claude site icon. Reused from the app's existing
  reviewed asset. Official source: https://claude.com/icon.png.
- `mcp.svg`: unmodified light-theme lockup from the official MCP repository,
  `docs/logo/light.svg`, retrieved 18 November 2026:
  https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/modelcontextprotocol/blob/main/docs/logo/light.svg.

The app's prior provenance is recorded in its
`documentation/development/connected-assistant-branding.md` (8 November 2026).
Marks remain the property of their respective owners. MCP is a protocol, not an
assistant; the visual and translated caption distinguish that role.

- `mistral.svg`: unmodified standalone gradient mark from the app's reviewed
  assistant assets. Official source: https://mistral.ai/brand/. The visible label
  uses Mistral Vibe without a subtitle. Codex CLI and custom
  tools use generic terminal/network symbols, not invented provider logos.
