Situations you might recognize

AssetFlow won't diagnose a fault, guarantee coverage, or promise savings — but it keeps the records, dates, and next steps together so you can act on them.

“I just bought a used car. What service is due, and when does the insurance renew?”

Add the car once, attach the documents you have, and confirm the suggested service and renewal dates so they show up when they matter.

“The appliance is making a noise. Can I find the receipt and warranty?”

Keep receipts, manuals, and warranty details linked to the appliance, so you're not searching through drawers or old emails.

“Did we already book the boiler service — or was that last year?”

A shared history of completed work means the household can check what was actually done, and when, instead of relying on memory.

“The bike needs attention, but I can't remember its last service.”

Log completed work as you go, so the next suggested task always starts from an accurate history.

“Should I repair this device or replace it? What have I already spent?”

Review recorded costs and history together as a starting point for the decision — the choice, and the outcome, stay yours.

How it works

  1. 1

    Add an asset

    Register what you own — a car, appliance, tool, or anything else you look after.

  2. 2

    Keep its documents and details together

    Attach receipts, manuals, warranties, and insurance so they live with the asset.

  3. 3

    Review suggested tasks and dates, then confirm

    Suggestions are drafts you review and approve — nothing is added automatically.

  4. 4

    Log completed work and use the history

    Recorded work becomes the history you rely on for the next decision.
