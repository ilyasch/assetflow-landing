Everyday questions. Clear next steps.

You stay in charge. AssetFlow turns the details you already have into a practical plan you can review, confirm, and keep up to date.

“Where is the receipt, and what needs doing next?”

Keep the document with the right asset, then see the next confirmed task without searching through drawers, inboxes, or memory.

“Did we already take care of this?”

Record completed work as it happens, so everyone in the household can see what was done and when.

“I bought a used car. What should I check first?”

Add the car, attach its service history, and review a smart maintenance plan before confirming the dates that matter to you.

“The washing machine is acting up. Can I find the warranty?”

Link the receipt, warranty, and notes to the appliance so the evidence is ready when you need it.

“What should we prepare before winter?”

Build a plan for the boiler, heat pump, bike, or home system, then choose what to confirm and when.

“Where are the solar-panel documents, and when should we check them?”

Keep the installation records and service notes with the home, then track the next review without relying on a folder or a reminder in your head.

Meet Flow AI, your assistant for asset records

Flow AI understands the records and completed work you add to each asset. Ask questions, uncover useful insights, and turn findings into suggested actions, tasks, or a smart maintenance plan—all for you to review.

  1. 01

    Ask about your records

    Get answers from the manuals, invoices, notes, and completed work you have saved with an asset.

  2. 02

    Find what matters

    Flow AI can bring relevant details and potential gaps in your records to your attention.

  3. 03

    Turn insight into action

    Use a finding to draft a task or build a smart maintenance plan, then review and adjust it before it becomes yours.

Flow AI suggestions are based on the information available in your records. You stay in control of every action.
